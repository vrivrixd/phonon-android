Download the `.apk` below and install it over the previous version.

## Changes

- The interface is now in English, with a Portuguese translation that follows the phone's language.
- Expressiveness options are listed from least to most expressive, with their temperature values.
- New "Measure response time" button: reports the time to first audio, the time to the first
  audible sound, and how much faster than real time speech is generated.
- New experimental "Faster audio decoder" option: runs the Mimi decoder in q8_0, which can shorten
  the delay before speech at a small cost in audio quality.
