Download the `.apk` below and install it over the previous version. Your settings are kept.

## Fixes

- Speed and pitch now work: the system and TalkBack speech rate and pitch, and the extra speed
  multiplier in the app. They had no effect in 1.0.
- A new default voice applies from the next utterance, without restarting TalkBack. A voice an app
  chose by name stays as the app chose it.
- Speech no longer stutters with the screen locked. Android was holding the engine to the slow
  processor cores; it now runs as a foreground service while in use, and works ahead of playback.
  On Android 12 and older this shows a quiet notification while the engine is in use.
- Changing the number of processor cores restarts the engine by itself, without closing the app
  or asking first. The engine now runs in its own process.

## New

- The app looks for a new version when it opens and offers to download and install it. Only this
  uses the internet; speech is still made entirely on the phone.
- An option to turn off battery optimization.
- More expressiveness values, from 0 to 3.0, with 1.0 recommended. The model's own 0.8 stays the
  default.
- Text in a language Phonon has no voices for is now read with an English voice, not a Portuguese
  one.
- Emoji are read aloud by name in all five languages, including skin tones, flags and combined
  emoji, with no added pauses. The names come from Unicode CLDR.
- The app lists the phone's language first, then English. On a phone in another language, English
  comes first.

## Removed

- The option to keep the silence before speech. It is now always trimmed.
