Download the `.apk` below and install it over the previous version, or update from the app. Your
settings are kept.

## New

- Eight new Portuguese (Brazil) voices: Amanda, Duda, Gabriel, Henrique, Leandro, Pablo, Rodrigo
  and Vrivri. They were contributed by volunteers for this app only; see
  `licenses/CONTRIBUTED-VOICES.txt`.
- Manage extra voices: add your own Phonon voice files (`.safetensors`), choosing the language each
  one speaks. Added voices show at the end of their language's list and can be used by any app.
  In the window, a tap plays a voice and a long press renames or deletes it. The files are copied
  into the app's own storage, so the originals can be deleted.
