Download the `.apk` below and install it over the previous version, or update from the app. Your
settings are kept. On first start the model is prepared again, which takes a few seconds.

## New

- Eight new Portuguese (Brazil) voices: Amanda, Arthur, Duda, Gabriel, Henrique, Leandro, Pablo and
  Rodrigo. They were contributed by volunteers for this app only; see
  `licenses/CONTRIBUTED-VOICES.txt`.
- Extra voices: add your own Phonon voice files (`.safetensors`) on the new Extra voices screen,
  choosing the language each one speaks. A tap plays a voice and a long press renames or deletes
  it. Added voices show at the end of their language's list, in alphabetical order, and any app
  can use them. The files are copied into the app's own storage, so the originals can be deleted.

## Changes

- The voices now sound as they do on the computer: the app uses the model's full-precision
  weights instead of a compressed copy. The APK is about 30 MB larger.
- The faint click before speech is gone. It came from the audio decoder starting up, and it also
  kept the silence after it from being trimmed, so speech now starts sooner too.
- Speech no longer stutters after the engine restarts while the app is in the background, for
  example while choosing a voice file.
